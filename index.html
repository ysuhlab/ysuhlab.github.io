Research Direction

Our research spans real-world domains including digital health, business intelligence, context-aware intelligent services, and AI-based design systems.


We aim to bridge data, models, and systems to create AI technologies that are both practically effective and scientifically grounded, with a focus on reliable and human-centered decision support.

Identification

Identify socially, economically, and culturally significant issues where data analytics and AI can create meaningful impact.

Analysis

Analyze real-world data and context to derive actionable insights and develop reliable AI models.

Prediction & Decision Support

Develop predictive and intelligent systems to support informed decisions and context-aware services.

Research Content

Our research advances data-centric AI, reliable evaluation, and intelligent systems—spanning both current projects and future research directions.

Data-centric AI &
Predictive Analytics

Data Quality & Labeling

Assess data quality and develop efficient preprocessing and labeling strategies.

Feature Engineering & Representation Learning

Design informative features and learn representations from diverse data.

Predictive Modeling & Anomaly Detection

Build predictive and anomaly-detection models, including for imbalanced data.

Reliable &
Explainable AI

Model & System Evaluation

Diagnose model and pipeline failures and develop metrics aligned with user outcomes.

Explainable AI

Interpret AI predictions and decisions using model explanation techniques.

Uncertainty Quantification & Calibration

Estimate uncertainty, calibrate confidence, and support selective prediction.

Generative AI &
Intelligent Systems

LLM & Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Develop grounded LLM applications, RAG pipelines, and domain-specific question answering.

Multimodal Learning & Fusion

Learn from and integrate text, images, sensors, and heterogeneous data.

Context Modeling & Adaptive Systems

Infer user and environmental context to develop adaptive intelligent services.
